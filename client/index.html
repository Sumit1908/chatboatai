WhatsApp Business API Platform for Teams | Sampark

Sampark is a WhatsApp Business API platform built for sales, support and marketing teams that want to turn conversations into revenue. Run broadcast campaigns, manage approved message templates, reply from a shared team inbox, and track delivery analytics in one dashboard — all on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. The product is designed for operators who already sell and support customers on WhatsApp and need a reliable business layer on top of personal chat apps.

Unlike unofficial bulk-sender tools, Sampark connects to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform so your number keeps a verified business profile, quality rating visibility, and compliant messaging limits. Teams across India use Sampark to launch offers, follow up leads, send order updates, and support customers where they already chat every day. Whether you run a D2C brand, a local service business, an education institute, or a multi-agent sales desk, the same workspace covers outbound campaigns and inbound replies without switching tools.

Why teams choose this WhatsApp Business API platform

Growing businesses need more than a personal WhatsApp handset. They need a platform that scales broadcasts safely, keeps history visible to the whole team, and shows whether each message was sent, delivered, read or failed. Sampark gives product, growth and support leads that operational clarity without forcing them to stitch together spreadsheets, personal phones, and grey-market gateways that risk number bans.

A typical team starts by connecting one dedicated business number, importing opted-in contacts, and submitting a first marketing or utility template for Meta review. Once approved, they can schedule a campaign, watch delivery metrics update live, and hand hot replies to agents in the shared inbox. Managers see the same conversation timeline as agents, which reduces dropped leads and duplicate outreach.

  • Broadcast campaigns to opted-in contacts with schedule and retry controls
  • Template manager for Meta submission, approval status and variables
  • Shared inbox so multiple teammates can reply on one WhatsApp number
  • Contact lists, tags and segments for precise WhatsApp marketing
  • Real-time campaign analytics for delivery and engagement
  • Workspace roles so owners and agents stay within clear permissions

Campaigns, inbox and analytics in one workspace

Outbound campaigns that stay compliant

Marketing and utility messages must use Meta-approved templates. Sampark's editor helps you structure headers, body variables, buttons and footer text, then tracks approval status so you are not guessing whether a template is live. Campaigns can target lists or tagged segments, and you can schedule sends for peak engagement windows in your market.

Shared inbox for the whole team

When customers reply, conversations land in one inbox. Agents can assign chats, keep context across shifts, and avoid the chaos of forwarding screenshots between personal phones. That is especially useful for high-intent leads who expect a fast human answer after a campaign.
